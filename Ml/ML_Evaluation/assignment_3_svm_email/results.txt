FINAL EVALUATION METRICS (TEST SET):
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Accuracy:              92.73% (0.9273)
Precision (Spam):      92.77% (0.9277)
Recall (Spam):         88.43% (0.8843)
F1 Score (Spam):       90.55% (0.9055)
Macro F1 Score:        92.32% (0.9232)

CONFUSION MATRIX SUMMARY:
----------------------------------------------------------------------
True Negatives (Legitimate Ham Correctly Allowed): 533
False Positives (Legitimate Ham Flagged as Spam): 25
False Negatives (Spam Leaked to Inbox):            42
True Positives (Spam Correctly Filtered):          321

SHORT INTERPRETATION OF RESULT:
----------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Predictive Reliability: The RBF-kernel SVM achieved an outstanding classification
   accuracy of 92.73% on unseen emails, confirming the effectiveness of margin
   maximization in high-dimensional word/character frequency feature spaces (57 dimensions).
2. Spam Precision vs. Recall: The model obtained a precision of 92.77% and a
   recall of 88.43% on spam detection. In email security, high precision is vital
   to prevent legitimate correspondence (ham) from being erroneously quarantined; our model
   demonstrated only 25 false positive errors out of 558 legitimate emails.
3. Importance of Scaling: StandardScaler was crucial because capital run-length attributes
   span values up to thousands, whereas word frequency percentages range from 0 to 100.
