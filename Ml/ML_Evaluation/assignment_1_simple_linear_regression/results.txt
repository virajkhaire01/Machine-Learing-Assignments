RESULT INTERPRETATION:
----------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Relationship: The negative coefficient of -0.0617 indicates an inverse
   relationship between engine displacement and fuel efficiency. For every 100 cubic
   inches increase in engine displacement, fuel economy decreases by approximately
   6.17 miles per gallon.
2. Goodness of Fit: The coefficient of determination (R² = 0.6633) demonstrates
   that approximately 66.33% of the variance in vehicle fuel economy is
   explained solely by engine displacement in this simple linear model.
3. Prediction Accuracy: The model yields a Root Mean Squared Error (RMSE) of 4.25
   MPG and a Mean Absolute Error (MAE) of 3.39 MPG on unseen test instances.
